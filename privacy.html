Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

This policy explains what we collect, why, and how you control it. We keep it simple and honest. If something is unclear, email us and we will explain.

1. What we collect

Account data

  • Your email address
  • Your username (auto-generated, editable)
  • A hashed password (we never see it in plain text)
  • Your date of birth (to verify you are 13+)
  • A timestamp of when you signed up
  • Your referral code (auto-generated), and who referred you if anyone

Content you create

  • Projects — the HTML, CSS, and JavaScript you write
  • Shop data — shop name, tagline, products, prices, delivery notes
  • Uploaded files — avatars, shop logos, product photos, chat attachments
  • AI chats — your prompts and the AI's replies
  • Chat messages between you and other KUMG users

Activity data

  • Which sponsored cards you tap (to credit your wallet)
  • When you use AI and how many messages you've sent today (to enforce limits)
  • Payment references when you upgrade to Pro
  • Basic feature usage events — which pages you open and which actions you take — so we can improve the app
  • Referral activity — who you invited and whether they created their first project

Device data (minimal)

  • Your theme preference (dark / light) — stored locally in your browser
  • Your language preference — stored locally in your browser
  • Your login session — stored locally in your browser

2. What we do not collect

  • Your location
  • Your contacts, SMS, phone log, or gallery (unless you actively pick a file to upload)
  • Your browsing history outside KUMG
  • Third-party advertising trackers

3. What is public vs private

Public anyone can see

  • Any website you publish (at its URL)
  • Any TouRryl Store you publish (name, tagline, products, prices, WhatsApp number)
  • Your avatar image (if you upload one)
  • Your referral code, if you share it
  • Sponsored cards you create if you are a business

Private only you

  • Your projects while unpublished
  • Your AI chat history
  • Your chat messages with other users — visible only to the two people in the conversation
  • Your KUMG Wallet balance and earnings
  • Your payment history
  • Your date of birth
  • Your email address
  • Your referral history (who you invited)

Site administrators can see aggregate counts and, for support purposes, your email and username. Admins cannot read your chat messages, wallet balance, unpublished projects, or AI conversation history.

4. Where your data lives

  • Authentication, database, files: Supabase (EU region)
  • Backend API: Render (EU region)
  • Frontend hosting: GitHub Pages
  • AI processing: Groq — your prompts are sent to their API for a reply and are not retained by Groq beyond the request
  • Image generation: Pollinations.ai — your prompt is sent to their service to generate the image
  • Video suggestions: Pexels — we link to their search results
  • Payments: Paynow (Zimbabwe) and manual EcoCash confirmation

5. How KUMG earns

KUMG is a real business. We keep the lights on through a few clear ways:

  • Pro subscriptions. $5/month for unlimited everything.
  • KUMG Tap. Sponsored cards from local businesses appear on your dashboard. When you tap them, you earn wallet credit and KUMG earns a share to run the app.
  • Future features. Paid tools and services we're building.

We do not sell your personal data to anyone. We do not run hidden advertising inside your AI chats, your messages, or your projects. Sponsored content is always labelled as sponsored.

6. How long we keep your data

  • Account and content data is kept until you delete your account.
  • AI chat history and image logs are kept for the life of your account, so you can review past conversations.
  • Chat messages are kept for the life of your account or until you or the other participant delete the chat.
  • Payment records are kept for 5 years, as required by Zimbabwean financial regulations.
  • Referral records are kept for the life of your account.
  • Basic usage events are kept for 12 months and then deleted.
  • When you delete your account, personal data is removed within 30 days. Aggregated anonymous data is kept.

7. Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's local storage for your theme, language, login session, and referral code only. We do not use third-party cookies. We do not track you across other websites.

8. Your rights

  • You can view and edit your profile at any time.
  • You can download your projects from the editor (Download HTML / ZIP).
  • You can delete any project, shop, product, chat, or AI conversation yourself.
  • You can remove your avatar from the Profile page.
  • You can delete your entire account from Settings. This removes all your personal data as described in section 6.
  • You can email us for a copy of your data.

9. Children

KUMG is not intended for children under 13. We ask for your date of birth at signup and refuse accounts under 13. If we learn that a child under 13 has signed up, we delete the account.

10. Security

Passwords are hashed. All data travels over HTTPS. Our database uses row-level security so each user can only access their own data. However, no system is perfectly secure — protect your password and log out on shared devices.

11. Changes to this policy

If we make significant changes, we will notify you inside the app before they take effect. Continued use after that means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact

Questions, data requests, or complaints? Email kumgtourryl@gmail.com. We respond within 5 working days.
