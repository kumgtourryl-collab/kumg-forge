Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

This policy explains what we collect, why, and how you can control it. We keep things simple and honest.

1. What we collect

  • Account data: your email, username, and hashed password (handled by Supabase Auth).
  • Content: the HTML, CSS, JS, images, and other files you create or upload.
  • Basic usage data: timestamps of signups, project creation, and payments.
  • Avatar: if you upload one, we store it in our storage bucket (publicly accessible by URL).

2. What we do NOT collect

  • We do not track your location.
  • We do not read your contacts, SMS, or photos (except images you actively upload).
  • We do not sell your data to third parties.
  • We do not run advertising trackers.

3. Where your data lives

  • Auth + database + files: Supabase (EU region).
  • Server-side API: Render (EU region).
  • Frontend hosting: GitHub Pages.

4. Who can see your data

  • Only you can see your profile, projects, and payment records (enforced by database policies).
  • Site admins can see aggregate counts (number of users, projects) and account emails for support.
  • Any site you publish is public — anyone with the URL can view it.
  • Your avatar image is public if you upload one.

5. Payments

Payments are processed by Paynow (Zimbabwe). We receive a payment reference and confirmation — but never your card number, CVV, or mobile money PIN.

6. Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's local storage to remember your theme (dark/light), language, and login session. We do not use third-party cookies.

7. Your rights

  • You can view, edit, or delete your account data at any time.
  • You can export your projects by downloading them from the editor.
  • To fully delete your account and all associated data, email us.

8. Children

KUMG Forge is not intended for children under 13. If we learn a child under 13 has signed up, we'll delete the account.

9. Security

Passwords are hashed. Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Database access is protected by Row Level Security. However, no system is perfectly secure — protect your password.

10. Contact

Questions or data requests? Email: kumgtourryl@gmail.com
